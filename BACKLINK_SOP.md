## ⚠️ Why This SOP Exists (Read This First)

This document covers **only** SEO tactics that:

1. **Google explicitly allows** in their webmaster guidelines
2. **Will not get joburgchurch.co.za penalised** for link manipulation
3. **Build real authority** rather than fake signals
4. **Are sustainable** for one person (you, the church owner) to maintain for 30 minutes per week

If anyone tells you to "auto-submit to 500 directories" or "buy 1000 backlinks for R200" — that person is trying to either sell you something or get your site deindexed. Close the tab.

---

## The Core Principle

A backlink is a vote of confidence from one site to another. Google ranks votes by:
- **Authority of the source** (a mention on BBC is worth 10,000 mentions on random blogs)
- **Relevance** (a link from a Christian church site is worth 10x a link from a casino site)
- **Context** (an editorial mention inside an article beats a footer link in a directory)
- **Diversity** (links from 50 different sources beat links from 1 source × 50)

Your job: get **5–10 genuinely good backlinks per month** from sources Google trusts.

## Tier 3: Content backlinks (slow but powerful, do 1 per week)

### Quora answers (highest ROI free content backlink)

1. Create a Quora profile: https://www.quora.com
2. Bio: "Mr. Sim leads the Wednesday Bible study at Johannesburg Bible Study Church in Sandton. https://joburgchurch.co.za"
3. Search for these questions once a week and answer ONE of them thoroughly:

> - "Where can I find a free Bible study in Johannesburg?"
> - "How do I learn Korean in South Africa?"
> - "What should I wear to church in Sandton?"
> - "Are there any Korean classes in Johannesburg?"
> - "What's the best church in Sandton for young adults?"
> - "How do I find a church near me in Johannesburg?"

Write a 200–400 word honest answer. End with "If you want to know more about what we do at our church, here is our site: https://joburgchurch.co.za/[relevant page]". **No spam. Just one answer a week.**

### Medium article reposts (one-time)

1. Create a Medium account: https://medium.com
2. Republish your 3 newest blog posts on Medium (one per week)
3. In each republish, add a link back to the original on joburgchurch.co.za
4. This is allowed (Medium permits reposts) and gives you a high-DA backlink

### LinkedIn articles (Mr. Sim or Ms. Dora)

1. Mr. Sim and/or Ms. Dora write a short (300-word) LinkedIn article about Bible study, faith, or Korean learning
2. End with a link to https://joburgchurch.co.za
3. Publish once a month

## ❌ What NOT To Do (the link spam trap)

If you see any of these being offered, walk away:

| Tactic | Why it's bad |
|--------|--------------|
| "Buy 1000 backlinks for R200" | Google penalises paid link schemes |
| "Submit to 500 directories automatically" | Most are spam farms Google ignores or devalues |
| "Auto-post to 100 forums with backlinks" | Footer link spam. Penalty bait. |
| "PBN (Private Blog Network) backlinks" | Google deindexes PBNs regularly |
| "Comment backlinks on random blogs" | Worthless since 2014 |
| "Article spinning for backlinks" | Google recognises spun content instantly |
| "Hire someone on Fiverr for SEO" | They will use black-hat tactics that burn your site |
| "Guest post network / link exchange" | Google's SpamBrain catches these |

A single black-hat link campaign can wipe out **months** of legitimate SEO work.

## When To Stop And Reassess

- After 6 months, if monthly organic clicks are still under 50, stop adding backlinks and instead invest in content depth
- After 12 months, if you are still under top 10 on Tier 1 keywords, the site may need a domain audit
- If GSC shows "manual action" or "unnatural links" warnings, **stop all backlink activity immediately** and audit
