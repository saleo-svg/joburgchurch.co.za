     line 6053+ so the orphan entries now sit inside the still-open
     posts object.

     that closes the now-complete 170-key posts object.
  3. Added a missing '};' to close the hymnRegionImages object.

     a single entry (one level of nested {}) so future promotions
     will not leak status/date edits into neighbouring entries.
