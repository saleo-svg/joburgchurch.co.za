The people of Israel have just walked out of Egypt. Pharaoh has changed his mind. Six hundred chariots are bearing down on them. And they are camped between the sea and the mountains with nowhere to go.

This is one of the most-quoted passages in the Old Testament, and one of the most misunderstood. Most people hear "God parted the Red Sea" and picture a quiet miracle. The text does not describe a quiet miracle. It describes a terrified people, an angry army, and a God who shows up at the last possible moment.
