# The Christian Attorney in Johannesburg: A South African's Guide to Law, Justice, Faith, and Working in the System You Are Called to Reform

## The Scene

The young attorney in Sandton graduated at the top of her class. She works at a firm that handles commercial law. She has never seen a legal aid case. She wonders whether her faith has anything to do with the work she does.

**2 Corinthians 12:9** — *"My grace is sufficient for you, for my power is made perfect in weakness."*

The South African Christian in the situations described above is not alone. The Bible has been speaking to these situations for two thousand years. The Wednesday Bible study in Parkmore has been hearing these passages read, discussed, and applied for ten years. What follows is a distillation of what the Bible says and what we have learned from the journey.


## What the Bible Says About Law and Justice

Micah 6:8 says: 'He has shown you, O mortal, what is good. And what does the Lord require of you? To act justly and to love mercy and to walk humbly with your God.' The attorney is in a profession that is built around justice. This is not a coincidence. The Christian attorney is called to act justly — not just within the law, but in the spirit of the law.

## The Tension Between Billable Hours and Justice

The South African legal profession operates on billable hours. The more hours billed, the more revenue. This creates a tension with justice — the person who cannot afford legal fees is less able to access justice. The Christian attorney is invited to find ways to provide pro bono service — not as charity, but as part of their calling.

## What the Christian Attorney Can Do

First: do excellent work in every case, regardless of the fee. Second: provide pro bono hours — most law societies require a minimum. Third: advocate for legal aid reform. Fourth: be known for integrity in a profession where integrity is not always the norm. Fifth: remember that the system you work in is not neutral. It reflects the values of the society that created it.

## A Prayer for the Christian Attorney

Father, we bring the attorney who works in a system that is complicated. We bring the one who is tempted to cut corners. We bring the one who is tempted to despair of justice. Give us the integrity that is your character. Give us the courage to act justly. Give us the wisdom to use the law as an instrument of your kingdom. Amen.


## A Closing Prayer

Father, we bring this struggle to you. We ask for your wisdom, your strength, and your presence. We ask for the community to hold us. We ask for the hope that does not disappoint. Amen.
