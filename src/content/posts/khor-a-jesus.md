## Khor a Jesus

*Khor a Jesus* (Voice of Jesus) is a traditional Dinka hymn from South Sudan. The Dinka people have a rich musical tradition, and Christian hymns have been adapted to reflect their cultural expressions of faith.

### Verse 1 (Dinka)
Khor a Jesus, raan a koc
Yee adit a maber
Khor a Kristo, wongona kene
Ngolona in jok arec

### Chorus (Chorus)
Yes, Maria! Maria!
Aleli, Maria, Maria
Moses kene ayaa!
Yen a Maria!

### Verse 2 (Dinka)
Koc a yotho, koc a de
Koc a dhogona mager
Yen a Kristo, wang wi
Ngolona in jok arec

### Translation Reference
- *Khor* = Voice / Sound
- *Jesus/Kristo* = Jesus / Christ
- *Maria* = Praise / Hallelujah
- *Koc* = Person / People
- *Yen* = He / He is
