# The Husband Who Wants to Lead But Doesn't Know How: A South African Christian's Honest Guide to Spiritual Leadership in the Home

## The Scene

The man in Randburg knows he should be leading his family spiritually. He goes to church. He reads his Bible occasionally. He has never led a family prayer. He is embarrassed by the idea. His wife has mentioned it twice. He has changed the subject both times.

**Hebrews 11:1** — *"Now faith is confidence in what we hope for and assurance about what we do not see."*

The South African Christian in the situations described above is not alone. The Bible has been speaking to these situations for two thousand years. The Wednesday Bible study in Parkmore has been hearing these passages read, discussed, and applied for ten years. What follows is a distillation of what the Bible says and what we have learned from the journey.


## What Ephesians 6 Actually Commands

Ephesians 6:4 says: 'Fathers, do not exasperate your children; instead, bring them up in the training and instruction of the Lord.' The command is not to be perfect. The command is to be present. The husband who is not leading at all is in violation of this command. The husband who is trying imperfectly is doing what the Bible commands.

## The Three Things Spiritual Leadership Is Not

Spiritual leadership is not: perfect behaviour (leadership is not about being perfect), daily devotionals with the family (this is a nice goal but not the minimum), or being the religious authority in the home. Spiritual leadership is: modelling faith (even imperfect faith), making the church a priority for the family, and being the person who holds the family together spiritually.

## The Minimum Viable Spiritual Leadership

Every husband can do these three things: First: lead the family to church — physically show up. Second: say grace before a meal occasionally — even if it is embarrassing at first. Third: have a regular one-on-one conversation with each child about faith — not a lecture, a conversation. These three things are not impressive. They are enough.

## A Prayer for the Husband Who Wants to Lead

Father, we bring the husband who is afraid to lead badly. We bring the man who knows he should and does not know how. We ask for the courage to try imperfectly. We ask for the wife who will not criticise the first attempt. We ask for the children who will be patient with their father. We ask for the grace that covers the failure and moves forward anyway. Make us leaders, Lord, even when we do not feel ready. Amen.


## A Closing Prayer

Father, we bring this struggle to you. We ask for your wisdom, your strength, and your presence. We ask for the community to hold us. We ask for the hope that does not disappoint. Amen.
