# When the Husband Won't Lead: A South African Christian Wife's Honest Guide to Submission, Leadership, and the God Who Sees Her

## The Scene

The woman in Roodepoort has been married for twelve years. Her husband is a good man. He provides. He is kind. He does not go to church. He does not pray. He has never opened a Bible. She has been praying for his salvation since before they were married.

**Romans 15:13** — *"May the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace as you trust in him, so that you may overflow with hope by the power of the Holy Spirit."*

The South African Christian in the situations described above is not alone. The Bible has been speaking to these situations for two thousand years. The Wednesday Bible study in Parkmore has been hearing these passages read, discussed, and applied for ten years. What follows is a distillation of what the Bible says and what we have learned from the journey.


## What Ephesians 5 Actually Says

Ephesians 5:21 says: 'Submit to one another out of reverence for Christ.' The word 'submit' comes after this mutual submission. The husband's leadership and the wife's submission in Ephesians 5 are set within the context of mutual submission. The wife's submission to an unsaved or uncooperative husband is not a blank cheque for his poor behaviour. It is a call to a particular kind of faithfulness.

## What 1 Peter Adds

1 Peter 3:1-2 says: 'Wives, in the same way submit yourselves to your husbands... Your beauty should not come from outward adornmentation... Rather, it should be that of your inner self, the unfading beauty of a gentle and quiet spirit, which is of great worth in God's sight.' The word 'gentle and quiet' does not mean passive or voiceless. It means a spirit that does not manipulate, coerce, or nag. The faithful wife wins her husband not by arguing but by the quality of her character.

## The Line Between Faithfulness and Enabling

There is a line between being a faithful wife and enabling a husband to avoid responsibility. If the husband will not work, the wife enabling him by working double shifts is not faithfulness. If the husband will not manage the household, the wife managing everything is not the answer. Each situation requires wisdom.

## A Prayer for the Faithful Wife

Father, we bring the wife who is tired of carrying what her husband will not lead. We bring the wife who prays alone. We bring the wife whose hope is in you alone. Give her the strength to be faithful. Give her the wisdom to know when to act and when to wait. Give her the hope that does not depend on his conversion. Give her the community to sustain her. Use her faithfulness as the instrument of his salvation. Amen.


## A Closing Prayer

Father, we bring this struggle to you. We ask for your wisdom, your strength, and your presence. We ask for the community to hold us. We ask for the hope that does not disappoint. Amen.
