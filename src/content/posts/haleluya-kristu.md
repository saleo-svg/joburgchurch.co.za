## Haleluya Kristu

*Haleluya Kristu* (Hallelujah Christ) is a Swahili praise song celebrating Jesus Christ as King. This song captures the joyful, rhythmic quality of East African Christian worship.

### Verse 1 (Kiswahili)
Haleluya Kristu! Mfalme wa wafalme
Haleluya Kristu! Yeye tu ni mwokozi
Kwa damu yake, alituondoa
Kwa upendo wake, tuko huru

### Chorus (Chorus)
Haleluya, haleluya!
Kristo ni baba yetu
Haleluya, haleluya!
Tukiwa pamoja na yeye

### Verse 2 (Kiswahili)
Alikuja duniani, kwa ajili yetu
Alipita mateso, kwa ajili yetu
A鱼基了, tangu mbinguni
Kifo chake, ni uhai wetu

### Translation Reference
- *Haleluya* = Hallelujah / Praise God
- *Kristu* = Christ
- *Mfalme wa wafalme* = King of Kings
- *Mwokozi* = Savior
- *Damu* = Blood
- *Mbinguni* = In Heaven
