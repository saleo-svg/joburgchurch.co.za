# Helping a Sandton Teenager Read the Bible: A Christian Parent's Guide to the Difficult Years of Faith

## The Scene

The fourteen-year-old in Midrand has started asking questions. 'Why does the Bible say that?' 'Why do we go to church?' 'Why do good people suffer?' The parent is quietly terrified.

**James 4:6** — *"God opposes the proud but shows favour to the humble."*

The South African Christian in the situations described above is not alone. The Bible has been speaking to these situations for two thousand years. The Wednesday Bible study in Parkmore has been hearing these passages read, discussed, and applied for ten years. What follows is a distillation of what the Bible says and what we have learned from the journey.


## Doubt Is Not the Enemy

The Bible is not afraid of questions. The Psalms are full of questions. The parent who is frightened by the teenager's questions has misunderstood the Bible.

## What the Bible Says About Raising Children

Proverbs 22:6: 'Start children off on the way they should go, and even when they are old they will not turn from it.' The parent starts. The parent does not finish. The teenager makes the faith their own.

## Practical Steps for the Parent

Keep reading the Bible yourself. Read together without making it an interrogation. Let the teenager ask the questions. Keep the door open. Pray specifically.

## A Word About the Church

The teenager who questions the Bible at home may also question the church. The parent is invited to distinguish between the faith and the church community. The faith is the inheritance. The church community is the vehicle.

## A Prayer for the Parent of the Questioning Teenager

Father, we do not have the answers. We have only the questions, and the teenagers who are asking them. Give us patience. Give us humility. Keep the Bible on the table. Keep the door open. Amen.


## A Closing Prayer

Father, we bring this struggle to you. We ask for your wisdom, your strength, and your presence. We ask for the community to hold us. We ask for the hope that does not disappoint. Amen.
