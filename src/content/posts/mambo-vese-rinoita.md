## Mambo Vese Rinoita

*Mambo Vese Rinoita* (All That I Need) is a Shona hymn from Zimbabwe that celebrates God's provision and care. Shona Christian music is known for its powerful harmonies and rhythmic complexity.

### Verse 1 (chiShona)
Mambo vese rinoita
Ndaona musoro wenyu
Ndinotenda ndinotenda
Mambo vese rinoita

### Chorus (Chorus)
Halleluya! Halleluya!
Mambo wedu rinoita
Halleluya! Halleluya!
Ndaona simba rake

### Verse 2 (chiShona)
Mabiko angu ari mberi
Ndinotenda ndinotenda
Ropa rangu rinoita
Jesu, wangu wega

### Translation Reference
- *Mambo* = King / Lord
- *Rinoita* = We have seen / We have found
- *Halleluya* = Hallelujah
- *Jesu* = Jesus
- *Simba* = Strength / Power
