## The Weekend the House Got Cleaned

There is a weekend in October in Johannesburg when the house gets cleaned. The cupboards get cleaned. The fridge gets cleaned. The garage gets cleaned. The garden shed gets cleaned. The closets get cleaned. The particular winter clothes get put away. The particular summer clothes get put out. The curtains get washed. The windows get washed. The garden furniture gets cleaned. The house gets cleaned.

The Christian in Sandton in 2026 has begun to think about the spring cleaning as a small picture of the renewal of the soul. The Bible has a great deal to say about cleaning. The Bible treats cleaning as one of the great Bible pictures.
