# The Grandparent Who Disciples: A South African Christian's Guide to the Theology of the Grandparent in 2026

## The Scene

The grandmother in Springs has been telling her grandson Bible stories for five years. He is eight. He knows the story of David and Goliath, of Noah, of the Exodus, of the Good Samaritan. He does not know how to pray. She is not sure which comes first.

**Psalm 92:12-14** — *"The righteous will flourish like a palm tree, they will grow like a cedar of Lebanon; planted in the house of the Lord, they will flourish in the courts of our God."*

The South African Christian in the situations described above is not alone. The Bible has been speaking to these situations for two thousand years. The Wednesday Bible study in Parkmore has been hearing these passages read, discussed, and applied for ten years. What follows is a distillation of what the Bible says and what we have learned from the journey.


## The Biblical Foundation for Grandparent Discipleship

Deuteronomy 4:9 says: 'Teach them to your children and their children after them.' The grandparent is explicitly in the chain of faith formation. Psalm 78:4 says: 'We will tell the next generation the praiseworthy deeds of the Lord.' The grandparent is called to be the keeper of the stories.

## The Specific Opportunities of the Grandparent

The grandparent has time that the parent does not. The grandparent has perspective that the parent does not. The grandparent has a love that is unconditional in a way that the parent's love is not. These are gifts the grandparent can use.

## What Discipleship Looks Like in the Grandparent Relationship

Telling the Bible stories. Praying together. Modelling the Christian life. Attending church with the grandchildren. Answering the hard questions. Being present at the milestones. The grandparent who disciples is the grandparent who shows up.

## A Prayer for the Grandparent Who Disciples

Father, we bring the grandparent who is forming the next generation. We ask for the wisdom to know what to say. We ask for the stories to be remembered. We ask for the prayers to be answered. We ask for the grandchildren to come to faith through the long, slow work of the grandparent. Amen.


## A Closing Prayer

Father, we bring this struggle to you. We ask for your wisdom, your strength, and your presence. We ask for the community to hold us. We ask for the hope that does not disappoint. Amen.
