## Mungu Ni Pema

*Mungu Ni Pema* (God Is Love) is a Swahili hymn that celebrates the unconditional love of God. Swahili-speaking Christians across East Africa have adopted this song as a beloved expression of faith.

### Verse 1 (Kiswahili)
Mungu ni pema, Mungu ni pema
Umeniokoa, uameniokoa
Kwa neema yake, kwa fahari yake
Nitakuwa daima, nitakuwa daima

### Chorus (Chorus)
Haleluya, haleluya
Mungu wa agano
Haleluya, haleluya
Sifa zako hazina kazi

### Verse 2 (Kiswahili)
Kwa dhambi yangu, ulitia damu
Kwa mateso yako, nimepata uzima
Ufalme wako, utakuja tena
Duka langu, litakuwa tayari

### Translation Reference
- *Mungu* = God
- *Pema* = Love
- *Haleluya* = Hallelujah
- *Neema* = Grace
- *Ufalme* = Kingdom
