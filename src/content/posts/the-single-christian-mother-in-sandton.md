# The Single Christian Mother in Sandton: A South African's Honest Guide to Raising Children Alone and Faithfully

## The Scene

It is six in the morning in Randburg. The mother is already up. The children are still asleep. She has already checked the bank balance on the phone, confirmed the lift from the neighbour, and laid out the school uniforms. She is alone again today. She has been alone for three years. She wonders sometimes whether God is paying attention.

**2 Corinthians 12:9** — *"My grace is sufficient for you, for my power is made perfect in weakness."*

The South African Christian in the situations described above is not alone. The Bible has been speaking to these situations for two thousand years. The Wednesday Bible study in Parkmore has been hearing these passages read, discussed, and applied for ten years. What follows is a distillation of what the Bible says and what we have learned from the journey.


## What the Bible Says About the Widow and the Orphan

The Bible has a special concern for the single parent. James 1:27 says: 'Religion that God our Father accepts as pure and faultless is this: to look after orphans and widows in their distress.' The single mother in Johannesburg is doing exactly what the Bible says matters most. She is looking after her children in distress. This is not a lesser Christian life. This is a holy Christian life.

Deuteronomy 10:18 says: 'He defends the cause of the fatherless and the widow.' God is on the side of the single mother. Not because she is weak. Because she is doing the work God has given her to do.

## The Church's Responsibility to the Single Mother

After ten years of Wednesday Bible study in Parkmore, we have learned that the single mother needs three things from the church: practical help, consistent presence, and theological affirmation. Practical help: meals after the birth, lifts to school, occasional childcare. Consistent presence: not a one-time visit, but an ongoing relationship. Theological affirmation: 'You are not being punished. You are not failing. You are doing holy work.'

## Raising Boys Without a Father in the Home

The single mother raising boys in Johannesburg faces a specific challenge. Proverbs 13:22 says: 'A good person leaves an inheritance to their children's children.' The single mother who is raising boys without a father figure in the home is not cursed. She is entrusted with an especially significant responsibility. The church community can help fill the gap — the older men who take the boys to the cricket, the youth group leaders who model male maturity.

## Dating as a Single Christian Mother

The single Christian mother in Johannesburg who is considering dating faces a uniquely difficult decision. 1 Corinthians 7:33-34 says the married person is concerned with pleasing their spouse, and the unmarried person with pleasing the Lord. The single mother who is also a Christian has three concerns: pleasing God, protecting the children, and managing her own loneliness. This is not a problem to be solved quickly. It requires prayer, counsel, and patience.

## Practical Ways to Support the Single Mother

First: practical help is more valuable than advice. Bring food. Give a lift. Mind the children for an afternoon. Second: financial dignity — if you want to give money, give it without conditions or strings. Third: respect her authority as a parent — do not undermine her decisions with her children. Fourth: remember the birthday — the single mother's birthday is often the loneliest day of the year. Fifth: pray specifically — not vague prayers, but named prayers for her specific situation.

## A Prayer for the Single Christian Mother

Father, we bring the single mother who is tired. We bring the mother who has carried this load alone for too long. We bring the mother who wonders if anyone sees. We declare that she is not alone. We declare that her work is holy. We ask for the practical provision she needs. We ask for the community to surround her. We ask for the children to grow in wisdom and love. We ask for her own heart to be refreshed. We ask for the church to be the hands and feet of Christ to her today. Amen.


## A Closing Prayer

Father, we bring this struggle to you. We ask for your wisdom, your strength, and your presence. We ask for the community to hold us. We ask for the hope that does not disappoint. Amen.
