# Remarrying After Divorce: A South African Christian's Honest Guide to Second Marriages, the Church's Judgment, and the God Who Gives Second Chances

## The Scene

The woman in Fourways has been divorced for three years. She is considering remarriage. Her church has not said anything directly, but she knows the culture. She is not sure whether she should ask the pastor's permission or just proceed.

**2 Corinthians 12:9** — *"My grace is sufficient for you, for my power is made perfect in weakness."*

The South African Christian in the situations described above is not alone. The Bible has been speaking to these situations for two thousand years. The Wednesday Bible study in Parkmore has been hearing these passages read, discussed, and applied for ten years. What follows is a distillation of what the Bible says and what we have learned from the journey.


## What the Bible Actually Says

1 Corinthians 7:10-16 addresses remarriage after divorce. Jesus in Matthew 19:3-9 addresses it directly. Both passages acknowledge that divorce happens, that remarriage may be permissible in some cases, and that the church should not be overly restrictive. The key teaching of Jesus is: 'What God has joined together, let no one separate.' But he also acknowledges that 'because your hearts were hard' Moses permitted divorce.

## The Hard Cases

Most pastors and theologians agree that remarriage is permissible in at least three cases: when the former spouse has committed adultery (Matthew 19:9), when the former spouse is an unbeliever who has deserted (1 Corinthians 7:15), and when there has been domestic violence or abuse. These are not easy cases. They require pastoral counsel.

## What the Church Should Not Do

The church should not condemn the person who remarries. The church should not make remarriage a confessional sin. The church should not treat the second marriage as lesser than the first. The church should provide pastoral counsel, not judgment.

## A Prayer for the Person Considering Remarriage

Father, we bring the second chance that feels like too much hope. We bring the person who has been told they do not deserve to try again. We ask for the wisdom to know what is right. We ask for the courage to act on it. We ask for the church to be a place of grace rather than judgment. We ask for the new marriage to be built on honesty and covenant. Amen.


## A Closing Prayer

Father, we bring this struggle to you. We ask for your wisdom, your strength, and your presence. We ask for the community to hold us. We ask for the hope that does not disappoint. Amen.
