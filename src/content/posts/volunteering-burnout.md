# The Volunteer Who Does Everything: A South African Christian's Honest Guide to Church Volunteering, Burnout, and Saying No to Good Things

## The Scene

The woman in Randburg leads the worship team, coordinates the welcome team, visits the hospital on Wednesdays, and bakes the cakes for the braai. She has been doing this for four years. She is exhausted. When she mentioned it to the pastor, the pastor asked her to take on the children's ministry too.

**1 Peter 4:10** — *"Each of you should use whatever gift you have received to serve others, as faithful stewards of God's grace in its various forms."*

The South African Christian in the situations described above is not alone. The Bible has been speaking to these situations for two thousand years. The Wednesday Bible study in Parkmore has been hearing these passages read, discussed, and applied for ten years. What follows is a distillation of what the Bible says and what we have learned from the journey.


## What 1 Peter 4 Says About Serving

1 Peter 4:10-11 says: 'Each of you should use whatever gift you have received to serve others, as faithful stewards of God's grace in its various forms.' The word 'steward' is important. A steward manages resources, including their own. The steward who gives away all their time and energy is not stewarding well.

## The Difference Between Serving and Enabling

The church that depends on one or two volunteers to do everything is a church that is burning those volunteers out. This is not the volunteers' fault. It is the church leadership's failure. The volunteer who is doing ten jobs is not a saint. They are a symptom of a church that has not distributed ministry broadly enough.

## Practical Steps for the Over-Volunteered Christian

First: count the hours. Write down every volunteer activity and the time it takes. Second: identify the two or three that give you energy — do those. Third: identify the ones that drain you — stop those. Fourth: have the conversation with leadership — not as a complaint but as a health check.

## A Prayer for the Over-Volunteered Christian

Father, we bring the volunteer who has said yes to everything and no to themselves. We bring the exhaustion that is the result of generosity without boundaries. Give us the wisdom to know what we should do and what we should not. Give us the courage to say no to good things so we can say yes to the best things. Give the church the grace to distribute ministry broadly. Amen.


## A Closing Prayer

Father, we bring this struggle to you. We ask for your wisdom, your strength, and your presence. We ask for the community to hold us. We ask for the hope that does not disappoint. Amen.
