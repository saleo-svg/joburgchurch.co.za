## Jesu, Usuqhamo Njani?

This Zulu worship song celebrates the greatness of God, translated from popular contemporary worship music. It reflects the vibrant praise tradition of African Christianity.

### Verse 1 (isiZulu)
Nkosi uyihlo kanye noMoya oyingcwele
Wadala amazulu, wenza konke okuhlelekile
Ungabikiwa, awufuni muntu akushade
Wena wedwa uyabonga, ungcwele wedwa

### Chorus (Chorus)
Jesu, usuqhamo njani?
Indlela yokuphila kwabantu bonke
Jesu, usuqhamo njani?
Uyingelezo lwanoma ngubani?

### Verse 2 (isiZulu)
Amandla, ubungcwele, ubukhosi bakho
Konke kuyakwamukela emoyeni
Ngiyabonga ngiyabonga ngiyabonga
Ngokuthula nangokuthula

### Translation Reference
- *Jesu* = Jesus
- *Usuqhamo njani* = How great You are
- *Nkosi* = Lord
- *Amandla* = Power
- *Uycwele* = Holy
