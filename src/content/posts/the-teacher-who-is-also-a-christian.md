# Teaching in Jesus' Name: A South African Christian Teacher's Honest Guide to Faith, Pedagogy, and the Classroom as a Mission Field

## The Scene

The woman who teaches Grade 7 in Springs has been teaching for twelve years. She is a Christian. She cannot lead the class in prayer. She cannot put a Bible on the desk. She wonders whether what she does in the classroom is faithful.

**James 1:5** — *"If any of you lacks wisdom, you should ask God, who gives generously to all without finding fault, and it will be given to you."*

The South African Christian in the situations described above is not alone. The Bible has been speaking to these situations for two thousand years. The Wednesday Bible study in Parkmore has been hearing these passages read, discussed, and applied for ten years. What follows is a distillation of what the Bible says and what we have learned from the journey.


## What the Bible Says About Teaching

Proverbs 1:7 says: 'The fear of the Lord is the beginning of knowledge.' The teacher who operates in fear of the Lord — who teaches with integrity, with care for the students, with excellence — is teaching from a Christian foundation even without naming it explicitly.

## The Classroom as a Mission Field

Matthew 28:19 says: 'Go and make disciples.' The teacher who is a Christian is already in the mission field. The students who have never encountered a person of faith may encounter one in the teacher who is kind when others are harsh, who is fair when the system is not, who is present for the struggling student. This is witness.

## Practical Faithfulness in the Classroom

First: teach excellently. Mediocre teaching in the name of Jesus is not witness. Second: treat every student as made in the image of God. Third: be honest about your faith when asked. Fourth: pray for the students by name. Fifth: maintain appropriate boundaries.

## A Prayer for the Christian Teacher

Father, we bring the teacher who stands in front of the class every day. We bring the one who is exhausted by the workload. We bring the one who wonders if any of it matters. Give us the excellence that is your character. Give us the patience that does not give up. Give us the students who will remember what we taught them. Use the classroom as your mission field. Amen.


## A Closing Prayer

Father, we bring this struggle to you. We ask for your wisdom, your strength, and your presence. We ask for the community to hold us. We ask for the hope that does not disappoint. Amen.
