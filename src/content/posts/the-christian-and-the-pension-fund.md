# Retiring Well in South Africa: A Christian's Guide to the Pension, the Property, and the Years That Remain

## The Scene

The man in Roodepoort turns sixty-five this year. The pension is R2.4 million. The financial advisor has done the math. The math is not encouraging.

**Matthew 6:26** — *"Look at the birds of the air; they do not sow or reap or store away in barns, and yet your heavenly Father feeds them. Are you not much more valuable than they?"*

The South African Christian in the situations described above is not alone. The Bible has been speaking to these situations for two thousand years. The Wednesday Bible study in Parkmore has been hearing these passages read, discussed, and applied for ten years. What follows is a distillation of what the Bible says and what we have learned from the journey.


## What the Bible Says About Aging

The Bible has a high view of aging. The older years are the years of wisdom, of fruitfulness, of testimony. Psalm 92:12-14: 'The righteous will flourish like a palm tree.'

## The Biblical View of Provision

The Bible does not promise the South African pension will be sufficient. The Bible does promise that God will provide. Matthew 6:26: 'Look at the birds of the air.'

## Practical Steps for the Pre-Retirement Years

Get professional financial advice early. Pay off the bond if possible. Plan for healthcare costs. Have the honest conversation with the family. Plan for purpose.

## Purpose in the Retirement Years

Titus 2:3-5 says older men and women are to teach what is good. The retirement years are not the years of rest from ministry. They are the years of a different kind of ministry.

## A Prayer for the Retirement Years

Father, we pray for those who are entering the retirement years with fear rather than hope. We ask for the financial wisdom to prepare well. We ask for the spiritual wisdom to prepare the heart. Amen.


## A Closing Prayer

Father, we bring this struggle to you. We ask for your wisdom, your strength, and your presence. We ask for the community to hold us. We ask for the hope that does not disappoint. Amen.
