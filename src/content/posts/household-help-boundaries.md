# Managing the Household Helper in Johannesburg: A South African Christian's Guide to Boundaries, Fairness, and the Employment Relationship in the Home

## The Scene

The woman in Sandton has an au pair who has become like family. She is not sure anymore where the employment relationship ends and the personal relationship begins. She has given her the evening off, but then she is stuck with the children. She wants to be generous. She also wants clear boundaries.

**2 Corinthians 12:9** — *"My grace is sufficient for you, for my power is made perfect in weakness."*

The South African Christian in the situations described above is not alone. The Bible has been speaking to these situations for two thousand years. The Wednesday Bible study in Parkmore has been hearing these passages read, discussed, and applied for ten years. What follows is a distillation of what the Bible says and what we have learned from the journey.


## What the Bible Says About Fair Employment

Colossians 4:1 says: 'Masters, provide your slaves with what is right and fair.' The principle of 'right and fair' applies to the domestic worker and the au pair as much as it applied to the ancient slave. This means paying the agreed-upon wage, providing the agreed-upon benefits, and not extending the workday beyond what was contracted.

## The Intimacy Problem

The domestic worker or au pair in the home is an employment relationship that is more intimate than most. The employer knows the worker's family situation, health, and personal life. The worker knows the employer's schedule, habits, and private moments. This intimacy creates both opportunity and danger. The opportunity is for genuine relationship. The danger is for exploitation of the intimacy by either party.

## Boundaries That Work

Clear boundaries are not unkind. They are kind. The employer who has clear working hours, clear duties, and clear communication is an employer who is easier to work for than one who is always adjusting. The au pair or domestic worker who knows what is expected of them can perform better than one who is guessing.

## A Prayer for the Employment Relationship

Father, we bring the employment relationship that is too intimate to be purely professional. We ask for fairness in the wage. We ask for respect in the communication. We ask for boundaries that protect both parties. We ask for the relationship to be a witness to your kingdom — to what employment can look like when it is done with justice and grace. Amen.


## A Closing Prayer

Father, we bring this struggle to you. We ask for your wisdom, your strength, and your presence. We ask for the community to hold us. We ask for the hope that does not disappoint. Amen.
