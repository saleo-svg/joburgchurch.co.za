# Faith on the Farm: A South African Christian Farmer's Guide to the Drought, the Locusts, the Rand, and the God Who Sends the Rain

## The Scene

The farmer in Free State has not seen meaningful rain in eight months. The cattle are thin. The maize is in the ground. The bank is calling.

**Matthew 6:26** — *"Look at the birds of the air; they do not sow or reap or store away in barns, and yet your heavenly Father feeds them. Are you not much more valuable than they?"*

The South African Christian in the situations described above is not alone. The Bible has been speaking to these situations for two thousand years. The Wednesday Bible study in Parkmore has been hearing these passages read, discussed, and applied for ten years. What follows is a distillation of what the Bible says and what we have learned from the journey.


## The Bible and the Farmer

James 5:7 says: 'Be patient, then, brothers and sisters, until the Lord's coming. See how the farmer waits for the land to yield its valuable crop, patiently waiting for the autumn and spring rains.' The farmer is the Bible's image of the patient one. The farmer who waits for the rain is the farmer who knows dependence on God.

## The Specific Challenges of the South African Farmer

The South African farmer in 2026 faces: drought, locust outbreaks, the cost of diesel for the irrigation pump, the cost of fertilizer, the cost of labour, the rand, the political environment. These are not minor challenges. They are existential. The farmer who has a faith in God is the farmer who can face these challenges with hope.

## What the City Christian Can Learn from the Farmer

The farmer is dependent on God in a way that the city Christian is not. The city Christian can buy food at the supermarket. The farmer cannot buy rain. The farmer is a teacher of dependence on God for the rest of the church.

## A Prayer for the Farmer

Father, we bring the farmer who is waiting for the rain. We bring the cattle that are thin. We bring the bank that is calling. We ask for the rain. We ask for the harvest. We ask for the faith to wait for the autumn and spring rains. We ask for the church to pray for the farmers in their midst. Amen.


## A Closing Prayer

Father, we bring this struggle to you. We ask for your wisdom, your strength, and your presence. We ask for the community to hold us. We ask for the hope that does not disappoint. Amen.
