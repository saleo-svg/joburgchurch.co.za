# When the Wedding Is Off: A South African Christian's Guide to Breaking the Engagement Without Breaking the Faith

## The Scene

The woman in Fourways called off the wedding in May. The deposit is lost. The invitations are in the cupboard. Her mother has not spoken to her in three weeks.

**2 Corinthians 12:9** — *"My grace is sufficient for you, for my power is made perfect in weakness."*

The South African Christian in the situations described above is not alone. The Bible has been speaking to these situations for two thousand years. The Wednesday Bible study in Parkmore has been hearing these passages read, discussed, and applied for ten years. What follows is a distillation of what the Bible says and what we have learned from the journey.


## The Courage to Stop the Wedding

Proverbs 19:21 says: 'Many are the plans in a person's heart, but it is the Lord's purpose that prevails.' The wedding that is not happening is not a failure of God's plan. It may be the prevention of a worse outcome.

## The Practical Unravelling

The cancelled wedding has a long tail of practical work. The deposits, the dress, the venue, the family. The Christian who has called off the wedding needs practical help. The church can help. Friends can help. The community can help.

## The Family Fallout

The cancelled wedding often creates family rupture. The mother who was looking forward to the wedding. The father who paid for the deposit. The siblings who are angry on behalf of the family. The Christian is called to bear the family anger without retaliating.

## A Prayer for the Person Who Called Off the Wedding

Father, we bring the courage that had to break what was once a hope. We bring the grief of the wedding that will not happen. We ask for the practical help to unmake the plans. We ask for the family to be patient. We ask for the future that is yours to give. Amen.


## A Closing Prayer

Father, we bring this struggle to you. We ask for your wisdom, your strength, and your presence. We ask for the community to hold us. We ask for the hope that does not disappoint. Amen.
