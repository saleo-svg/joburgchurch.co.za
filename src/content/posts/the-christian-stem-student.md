# Faith in the Laboratory: A South African Christian STEM Student's Guide to Science, Faith, and the God Who Created the Universe You Are Studying

## The Scene

The engineering student in Pretoria has been told by his lecturer that faith is incompatible with science. He is a Christian. He is also a serious student. He does not know how to hold both.

**Genesis 1:31** — *"God saw all that he had made, and it was very good."*

The South African Christian in the situations described above is not alone. The Bible has been speaking to these situations for two thousand years. The Wednesday Bible study in Parkmore has been hearing these passages read, discussed, and applied for ten years. What follows is a distillation of what the Bible says and what we have learned from the journey.


## The False Conflict

The perceived conflict between faith and science is, historically, a recent invention. The founders of modern science — Newton, Kepler, Faraday, Maxwell — were all devout Christians. The study of the natural world was, for centuries, called 'natural theology' — the study of God through his creation.

## What Genesis 1 Actually Says

Genesis 1:1 says: 'In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth.' The verse affirms that there is a God and that he created. It does not specify the mechanism. The Christian is free to study the mechanism — biology, chemistry, physics — without contradicting the theology. The two are different questions.

## Practising Faith in the Laboratory

First: do excellent work. The work itself is a witness. Second: do not be afraid of the hard questions. The faith that cannot survive the hard questions is the faith that has not yet grown. Third: find a community of Christian scientists — they exist in South Africa, in places like the STEM Christians South Africa network. Fourth: pray about your work. The work is not separate from the prayer.

## A Prayer for the Christian Scientist

Father, we bring the one who studies the world you made. We ask for the humility to learn what the universe is teaching us. We ask for the courage to hold faith and science together. We ask for the community of other Christian scientists. We ask for the wisdom to know that you are bigger than either. Amen.


## A Closing Prayer

Father, we bring this struggle to you. We ask for your wisdom, your strength, and your presence. We ask for the community to hold us. We ask for the hope that does not disappoint. Amen.
