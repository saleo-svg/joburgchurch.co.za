## The Season That No One Wants to Talk About

There is a season in the life of many South African Christians that is rarely discussed in church. It is the season of singleness. It is the season of being an adult in Johannesburg who is not married, not dating, not in a serious relationship, not in the place the wider culture assumes an adult should be.

The season can be by choice. The season can be by circumstance. The season can be by grief. The season can be by vocation. The season can be by health. The season can be by a long discernment that has not yet concluded.

The season is the same regardless of how it began. The season is the long wait. The season is the in-between. The season is the time when the world asks the question *Why are you still single?* and the answer is not a single sentence.

This post is for the single adults in Johannesburg. It is also for the church communities that want to love the single adults better. It is honest about the hard parts. It is practical about the everyday parts. It is rooted in the Bible.

## What the Bible Says About Singleness

The Bible has a lot to say about singleness. The Bible has a lot more to say than you might expect.

**1 Corinthians 7:7-8** — *I wish that all of you were as I am. But each of you has your own gift from God; one has this gift, another has that. Now to the unmarried and the widows I say: It is good for them to stay unmarried, as I do.*

**1 Corinthians 7:32-35** — *I would like you to be free from concern. An unmarried man is concerned about the Lord's affairs — how he can please the Lord. But a married man is concerned about the affairs of this world — how he can please his wife — and his interests are divided. An unmarried woman or virgin is concerned about the Lord's affairs: Her aim is to be devoted to the Lord in both body and spirit. But a married woman is concerned about the affairs of this world — how she can please her husband. I am saying this for your own good, not to restrict you, but so that you may live in a right and proper way in undivided devotion to the Lord.*

**Matthew 19:12** — *For there are eunuchs who were born that way, and there are eunuchs who have been made eunuchs by others — and there are those who choose to live like eunuchs for the sake of the kingdom of heaven. The one who can accept this should accept it.*

**Luke 2:36-37** — *There was also a prophetess, Anna, the daughter of Phanuel, of the tribe of Asher. She was very old; she had lived with her husband seven years after her marriage, and then was a widow until she was eighty-four. She never left the temple but worshipped night and day, fasting and praying.*

**Romans 12:2** — *Do not conform to the patterns of this world, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind. Then you will be able to test and approve what God's will is — his good, pleasing and perfect will.*

What you notice when you read these verses together is that singleness is not presented as a problem to be solved. Singleness is presented as a gift to be received. The gift is not the same for everyone. The gift is the gift of undivided attention to the things of the Lord. The gift is the gift of a particular kind of life. The gift is the gift of a particular season.

The single adult in Johannesburg who has learned to honour the season has learned to receive the gift. The single adult who has not learned to honour the season has learned to endure the waiting. The two are very different.

## The Five Temptations of the Single Adult in Johannesburg

The single adult in Johannesburg faces five particular temptations. The temptations are not the same as the single adult in Cape Town, or in Pretoria, or in Durban. The temptations are specific to the City of Gold.

### Temptation 1 — The Comparison Trap

The comparison trap is the comparison of your life to the lives of your married friends. The marriage is on Instagram. The marriage is on Facebook. The marriage is at the wedding you are invited to every other weekend. The marriage is at the baby shower you are invited to every other month.

The comparison trap is not the same as the comparison trap of ten years ago. The comparison trap of ten years ago was the comparison you did in your own head. The comparison trap of today is the comparison the social media does for you. The feeds are curated. The photos are edited. The stories are the highlight reel. The married life is not actually what the social media shows. The marriage is also a long obedience. The marriage is also a hard work. The marriage is also a season of waiting.

The Bible is not silent about the comparison trap. The Bible says in 2 Corinthians 10:12 — *We do not dare to classify or compare ourselves with some who commend themselves. When they measure themselves by themselves and compare themselves with themselves, they are not wise.*

### Temptation 2 — The Compromise Trap

The compromise trap is the temptation to settle for the wrong relationship because the right relationship has not arrived. The compromise trap is the temptation to date the wrong person because the right person is still in the future. The compromise trap is the temptation to marry the wrong person because the marriage is the only way to end the singleness.

The compromise trap is particularly strong in Johannesburg. The dating pool in Johannesburg is small. The Christian dating pool in Johannesburg is smaller. The Christian dating pool in Johannesburg who is also the right age, the right stage of life, the right faith, the right values, the right direction — that pool is very small.

The Bible is not silent about the compromise trap. The Bible says in 2 Corinthians 6:14 — *Do not be yoked together with unbelievers. For what do righteousness and wickedness have in common? Or what fellowship can light have with darkness?*

The verse is not saying that the unmarried Christian must marry. The verse is saying that the unmarried Christian should not marry the wrong person. The patience is worth it. The waiting is worth it. The compromise is not worth it.

### Temptation 3 — The Busyness Trap

The busyness trap is the temptation to fill the singleness with endless activity. The busyness trap is the temptation to overwork, to over-volunteer, to over-socialise, to over-travel, to over-extend. The busyness trap is the temptation to use the schedule to avoid the question.

The busyness trap is particularly strong in Johannesburg. The city is full of things to do. The city is full of events to attend. The city is full of people to meet. The city is full of opportunities to take. The busyness is the air that the city breathes.

The Bible is not silent about the busyness trap. The Bible says in Psalm 46:10 — *Be still, and know that I am God.*

The verse is the verse the single adult most needs to hear. The still is the opposite of the busyness. The still is the place where the singleness becomes the season. The still is the place where the question is asked in the quiet. The still is the place where the answer is given in the quiet.

### Temptation 4 — The Self-Reliance Trap

The self-reliance trap is the temptation to do life alone. The self-reliance trap is the temptation to build the life that does not need anyone. The self-reliance trap is the temptation to be the strong one. The self-reliance trap is the temptation to be the one who does not ask for help.

The self-reliance trap is particularly strong in Johannesburg. The city is a city of self-reliance. The city is a city of do-it-yourself. The city is a city of pull-yourself-up. The city is a city of strong people.

The Bible is not silent about the self-reliance trap. The Bible says in Galatians 6:2 — *Carry each other's burdens, and in this way you will fulfil the law of Christ.*

The verse is the verse that the self-reliant single adult most needs to hear. The burdens are not meant to be carried alone. The burdens are meant to be carried by the community. The community is the one who holds the single adult in the hard season. The community is the one who comes to the house when the single adult is sick. The community is the one who shows up at the hospital. The community is the one who eats the meal at the wedding. The community is the one who does the long obedience of friendship when the marriage has not yet come.

### Temptation 5 — The Identity Trap

The identity trap is the temptation to make the singleness the whole identity. The identity trap is the temptation to introduce yourself as *the single one*. The identity trap is the temptation to let the relationship status define the person.

The Bible is not silent about the identity trap. The Bible says in 1 Peter 2:9 — *But you are a chosen people, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, God's special possession, that you may declare the praises of him who called you out of darkness into his wonderful light.*

The identity is not the relationship status. The identity is the chosen people. The identity is the royal priesthood. The identity is the holy nation. The identity is the one who has been called out of darkness into the light. The single adult is, first and foremost, the child of God. The single adult is, first and foremost, the disciple of Jesus. The single adult is, first and foremost, the salt of the earth and the light of the world. The relationship status is the second thing. The relationship status is the second thing.

## The Three Practices That Have Helped the Single Adults in Our Bible Study

Over the last three years, the single adults in our Wednesday Bible study community have shared the practices that have helped them in the long wait. These three practices have come up again and again.

### Practice 1 — Keep the Sabbath

The practice of the Sabbath is the practice of stopping. The Sabbath is the practice of the seventh day. The Sabbath is the practice of the day you are not producing. The Sabbath is the practice of the day you are not performing. The Sabbath is the practice of the day you are not dating.

The single adult who has learned to honour the Sabbath has learned to honour the season. The Sabbath is the weekly reminder that the identity is not the schedule. The Sabbath is the weekly reminder that the worth is not the productivity. The Sabbath is the weekly reminder that the life is not the doing.

The single adult in Johannesburg who tries to keep the Sabbath in a city that does not stop has learned to keep the Sabbath in a small way. The Saturday morning walk. The Sunday afternoon quiet. The Friday evening at the Bible study. The lazy Sunday morning coffee. The Sabbath is the frame that holds the week.

### Practice 2 — Build the Friendship First

The single adult who has learned to honour the season has learned to build the friendship first. The friendship is the foundation. The friendship is the practice. The friendship is the long obedience.

The friendship is the one who shares the meal. The friendship is the one who comes to the house. The friendship is the one who calls on the birthday. The friendship is the one who shows up at the hospital. The friendship is the one who prays for the friend. The friendship is the one who holds the friend in the long wait.

The single adult who has built the friendship has built the foundation for the marriage that may come. The single adult who has built the friendship has also built the foundation for the life that does not include the marriage. Both are real. Both are honoured.

### Practice 3 — Serve Beyond Yourself

The single adult who has learned to honour the season has learned to serve beyond the self. The single adult has the time. The single adult has the energy. The single adult has the bandwidth. The single adult is, in a paradoxical way, the one who has more to give than the married adult who has the family.

The serving is the volunteering at the church. The serving is the mentoring of the young adult. The serving is the cooking of the meal for the friend with the new baby. The serving is the driving of the elderly parent to the doctor. The serving is the welcoming of the new person at the Bible study. The serving is the long obedience of the practical love.

The serving is not the compensation for the marriage that has not come. The serving is the practice of the Christian life that the singleness has made possible.

## A Prayer for the Single Adult in Johannesburg

If you are the single adult in Johannesburg — the one who is waiting, the one who is wondering, the one who is holding the season — here is a prayer.

> *God of the long wait,*
>
> *We thank you for the life you have given us. We thank you for the work. We thank you for the friends. We thank you for the city. We thank you for the Bible study. We thank you for the Wednesday night.*
>
> *We name the waiting. We do not pretend the waiting is easy. We name the questions we have asked. We name the comparison we have done. We name the compromise we have been tempted to. We name the busyness we have used. We name the self-reliance we have leaned on. We name the identity we have made of the singleness.*
>
> *We ask you to forgive us for the times we have settled for less than your best. We ask you to forgive us for the times we have made the singleness the whole story. We ask you to forgive us for the times we have refused the gift.*
>
> *We ask you to give us the grace of the season. We ask you to give us the friends who will hold us. We ask you to give us the work that uses our gifts. We ask you to give us the practice of the Sabbath. We ask you to give us the long obedience of the friendship.*
>
> *Whether the marriage comes or does not come, we ask you to make us the disciple you have called us to be. We ask you to make us the salt of the city. We ask you to make us the light of the city.*
>
> *In the name of the one who was unmarried and whole, and who loved the church as his bride.*
> *Amen.*
