# Faith and Medicine: A South African Christian Pharmacist's Honest Guide to Prescription Drugs, Natural Remedies, and the God Who Heals Through Both

## The Scene

The woman in Roodepoort has been on antidepressants for two years. Her pastor suggested she come off them. Her doctor said she should stay on them. She is not sure who to believe. She is not sure what her faith requires of her.

**2 Corinthians 12:9** — *"My grace is sufficient for you, for my power is made perfect in weakness."*

The South African Christian in the situations described above is not alone. The Bible has been speaking to these situations for two thousand years. The Wednesday Bible study in Parkmore has been hearing these passages read, discussed, and applied for ten years. What follows is a distillation of what the Bible says and what we have learned from the journey.


## The Biblical Foundation

Luke 10:34 describes the Good Samaritan who 'bandaged his wounds, pouring on oil and wine.' The oil and wine were medical treatment. Jesus commended this action without suggesting that prayer alone would have been sufficient. The Bible does not oppose medicine. The Bible assumes it.

## The Dangerous Idea That Faith Means No Medicine

Some Christian traditions have taught that taking medicine is a lack of faith. This teaching is not supported by the Bible. It has caused real harm. The Christian who stops taking life-sustaining medicine because a faith healer told them to is a Christian who has been deceived by false teaching.

## The Christian Who Cannot Afford Medicine

The Christian in South Africa who cannot afford their medication faces a specific injustice. The chronic medication for HIV, for diabetes, for hypertension is expensive. The state system is overstretched. This is a justice issue that the church should care about.

## A Prayer for the Person Taking Medicine

Father, we bring the medicine we take every day. We bring the shame of taking it. We bring the gratitude for it. We ask for the wisdom to use medicine well — as your gift, not as our idol. We ask for the healthcare workers who prescribe and dispense to do so with care. We ask for the grace to believe that medicine and faith are not enemies. Amen.


## A Closing Prayer

Father, we bring this struggle to you. We ask for your wisdom, your strength, and your presence. We ask for the community to hold us. We ask for the hope that does not disappoint. Amen.
