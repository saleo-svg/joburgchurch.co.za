## Koc na Ngun

*Koc na Ngun* (God Is With Us) is a Dinka worship song expressing the assurance of God's presence. In South Sudanese Christian worship, songs about God's presence bring comfort and hope to communities that have experienced conflict and displacement.

### Verse 1 (Dinka)
Koc na Ngun, yen a rec
Ayanu kere kere
Koc na Ngun, yen a maber
Ngolona in kamaic

### Chorus (Chorus)
Aleli! Aleli!
Maria nga Ngun
Aleli! Aleli!
Ngolona in jok arec

### Verse 2 (Dinka)
Jok arec, ayaa kene
Ngolona maber a nyoroc
Adit a maber, cik a nyoroc
Koc na Ngun, yen a maber

### Translation Reference
- *Koc na Ngun* = God is with us
- *Aleli* = Praise
- *Maria* = Hallelujah
- *Jok arec* = All the people
- *Maber* = Good / Mercy
