# The Whisper Campaign: A South African Christian's Honest Guide to Gossip, Rumour, and the Destruction of Reputation in the Church

## The Scene

The man in Fourways heard something about a member of the congregation. He mentioned it to his wife. His wife mentioned it to her friend. By Friday it had reached the person it was about, in a form that bore little resemblance to the original. The man heard it back and did not recognise it as his own words.

**Proverbs 12:22** — *"The Lord detests lying lips, but he delights in people who are trustworthy."*

The South African Christian in the situations described above is not alone. The Bible has been speaking to these situations for two thousand years. The Wednesday Bible study in Parkmore has been hearing these passages read, discussed, and applied for ten years. What follows is a distillation of what the Bible says and what we have learned from the journey.


## What the Bible Says About Gossip

Proverbs 26:20 says: 'Without wood a fire goes out; without a gossip a quarrel dies down.' Proverbs 18:8 says: 'The words of a gossip are like choice morsels; they go down into the most inner parts of the body.' The Bible treats gossip as serious. It is listed alongside murder, theft, and adultery in the catalogue of sins in Romans 1:29-30.

## The Anatomy of a Gossip Culture

Gossip culture in a church does not start with one person who gossips. It starts with a culture where information is currency, where closeness is measured by what you know about others, and where accountability is absent. The church that has no regular teaching on speech will inevitably develop a gossip culture.

## How to Stop Gossip in Your Own Speech

Before speaking about someone else, ask five questions: Is it true? Is it necessary? Is it kind? Is it the whole picture? Would I say this if they were here? These five questions eliminate most gossip. James 1:19 says: 'Everyone should be quick to listen, slow to speak and slow to become angry.'

## A Word to the Person Being Gossiped About

The person who is the subject of church gossip faces a specific challenge. Proverbs 26:21 says: 'As charcoal to embers and wood to fire, so is a quarrelsome person for kindling strife.' The response to gossip is rarely effective when it is direct confrontation. The most effective response is silence and a changed life.

## A Prayer for the Church That Gossips

Father, we confess the gossip that has been spoken in your name. We confess the reputations we have damaged with our tongues. We confess the secrets we have shared that were not ours to share. Give us discipline over our speech. Give us the courage to stop the whisper by not passing it on. Give us the love that builds up rather than tears down. Make our speech a witness to your kingdom. Amen.


## A Closing Prayer

Father, we bring this struggle to you. We ask for your wisdom, your strength, and your presence. We ask for the community to hold us. We ask for the hope that does not disappoint. Amen.
