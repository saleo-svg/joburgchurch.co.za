## The Long Way from Granny's House

There was a time, not very long ago, when the Christian grandparent in Johannesburg was not a difficult subject. The Christian grandparent lived in the same suburb as the grandchildren, or in the same township, or in the same province. The grandchildren came to granny's house on a Sunday afternoon. The grandchildren sat on granny's lap. The grandchildren learned the Bible from granny's voice. The grandchildren learned the psalms from granny's singing. The grandchildren learned the prayers from granny's example. The Christian grandparent in Johannesburg in 1986 was a ministry by proximity. The proximity did most of the work.

The Christian grandparent in Johannesburg in 2026 is a different kind of ministry. The grandchildren live in Cape Town. The grandchildren live in Durban. The grandchildren live in Pretoria, or in London, or in Sydney, or in Dubai. The grandchildren live two suburbs away, but the two suburbs are an hour's drive in the M1 traffic on a Saturday morning. The grandchildren live with both parents, who are both working, who both have a phone, who both have a WhatsApp group that is more present in the grandchildren's life than the grandparent is. The grandchildren live in a world of screens, where the screen has a louder voice than the grandparent does. The Christian grandparent in Johannesburg in 2026 has a calling that is harder than the calling in 1986. The calling has not been withdrawn. The calling has been given a longer road.

This post is for the Christian grandparent in Johannesburg in 2026. It is for the grandmother in Parkmore who sees her grandchildren three times a year.
