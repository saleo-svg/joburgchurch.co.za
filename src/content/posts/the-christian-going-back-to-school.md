# Back to School at Forty-Five: A South African Christian's Guide to the Adult Learner Returning to Education

## The Scene

The woman in Sandton has enrolled in a part-time degree at the University of Johannesburg. She is forty-seven. Her classmates are twenty. The first assignment is due in three weeks.

**Romans 5:3-4** — *"Not only so, but we also glory in our sufferings, because we know that suffering produces perseverance; perseverance, character; and character, hope."*

The South African Christian in the situations described above is not alone. The Bible has been speaking to these situations for two thousand years. The Wednesday Bible study in Parkmore has been hearing these passages read, discussed, and applied for ten years. What follows is a distillation of what the Bible says and what we have learned from the journey.


## The Bible and the New Thing

Isaiah 43:19 says: 'See, I am doing a new thing! Now it springs up; do you not perceive it?' The Christian who goes back to school at forty-five is doing a new thing. The new thing is not always easy. It is usually a sign of the Spirit's work.

## The Specific Challenges of the Adult Learner

The adult learner in the South African university in 2026 faces specific challenges. The fees. The technology (the LMS that was built for twenty-year-olds). The younger classmates. The competing demands of work and family. These are not impossible. But they require intentionality.

## What Helps the Adult Learner Succeed

First: a clear purpose. Know why you are doing this. Second: a community of support — family, friends, study group. Third: a realistic schedule that builds in margin. Fourth: grace for the mistakes. The adult learner who gives up after the first failed assignment has not yet learned to learn from failure.

## A Prayer Before the First Assignment

Father, we bring the back-to-school decision. We ask for the wisdom to manage the time. We ask for the community to support. We ask for the grace to fail and try again. We ask for the degree that is the goal. Most of all, we ask for the new thing you are doing to be visible. Amen.


## A Closing Prayer

Father, we bring this struggle to you. We ask for your wisdom, your strength, and your presence. We ask for the community to hold us. We ask for the hope that does not disappoint. Amen.
