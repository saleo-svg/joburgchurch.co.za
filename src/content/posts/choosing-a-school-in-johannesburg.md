# The School Decision: A South African Christian's Practical Guide to Choosing a School for Your Child in Johannesburg in 2026

## The Scene

The mother in Randburg has visited seven schools. She has a spreadsheet. She has colour-coded the criteria. The decision is due on Friday. She is still unsure. Every school has something wrong with it. No school is perfect. She does not want to make the wrong choice.

**James 1:5** — *"If any of you lacks wisdom, you should ask God, who gives generously to all without finding fault, and it will be given to you."*

The South African Christian in the situations described above is not alone. The Bible has been speaking to these situations for two thousand years. The Wednesday Bible study in Parkmore has been hearing these passages read, discussed, and applied for ten years. What follows is a distillation of what the Bible says and what we have learned from the journey.


## What the Bible Says About Education

Proverbs 1:7 says: 'The fear of the Lord is the beginning of knowledge.' The question 'is this a Christian school?' is not the only question. It is an important question. But it is not the only question. A school that calls itself Christian but has poor academics, poor discipline, or a toxic culture may not be the best environment for a child who is learning to love God.

## The Three Things That Actually Matter

After ten years of Wednesday Bible study in Parkmore, and many conversations about school decisions, we have learned that three things matter most. First: the quality of the teachers. A great teacher in an average school is worth more than an average teacher in a great school. Second: the culture of the school — what do the children look like, how do they treat each other, how do they treat the teachers. Third: proximity. A school that is close enough to get to easily will make the child's and the family's life significantly better.

## The Particular Burden of the Private School Decision

Many Johannesburg parents are making school decisions they cannot really afford. The private school fees in 2026 are significant. Proverbs 21:17 says: 'Whoever loves pleasure will become poor.' The parent who chooses the most expensive school they can barely afford is often making a decision based on status rather than wisdom. The child does better in a school the family can afford without stress.

## When You Have Made the Wrong Choice

The parent who has made a school decision that is not working is invited to change it. Not every school decision is reversible. But many are. The child who is struggling in the wrong school is not being faithful to a bad decision. They are being harmed by it.

## A Prayer Before the School Decision

Father, we bring the school decision that feels too important to get wrong. We ask for wisdom beyond our own. We ask for the peace that comes from making the best decision we can and then letting go. We ask for the flexibility to change course if we have chosen wrong. We ask for the grace to support our children wherever they go to school. We ask for the school to be a place of growth, community, and safety. Amen.


## A Closing Prayer

Father, we bring this struggle to you. We ask for your wisdom, your strength, and your presence. We ask for the community to hold us. We ask for the hope that does not disappoint. Amen.
