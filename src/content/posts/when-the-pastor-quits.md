# When the Pastor Resigns: A South African Christian's Honest Guide to Leadership Transitions, Grief, and the God Who Is Still in Charge

## The Scene

The church in Randburg has just learned that the pastor of seven years has resigned. The reasons are unclear. The community is in shock. The Sunday service is next week.

**1 Thessalonians 4:13** — *"Brothers and sisters, we do not want you to be uninformed about those who sleep in death, so that you do not grieve like the rest of mankind, who have no hope."*

The South African Christian in the situations described above is not alone. The Bible has been speaking to these situations for two thousand years. The Wednesday Bible study in Parkmore has been hearing these passages read, discussed, and applied for ten years. What follows is a distillation of what the Bible says and what we have learned from the journey.


## What the Bible Says About Pastors and Transitions

Acts 20:17-38 records Paul's farewell to the Ephesian elders. He warned them that after his departure, 'savage wolves will come in among you.' Pastoral transitions are dangerous moments for churches. The church that handles the transition well will be strengthened. The church that handles it badly will be damaged.

## The Common Reactions

Grief, anger, denial, bargaining, blame. These are the five stages of any significant loss. The church that has lost its pastor is experiencing a loss. The community is invited to acknowledge the loss rather than rush past it.

## What the Transition Is and Is Not

The transition is not the end of the church. The church is the people, not the pastor. The transition is a time to recommit to the church's mission. The transition is also a time to be honest about what was good and what was not about the previous pastor's ministry.

## A Prayer in the Pastoral Transition

Father, we bring the church that is in transition. We bring the grief of the pastor's leaving. We bring the uncertainty of what comes next. We ask for the interim leadership to be wise. We ask for the next pastor to be called by you. We ask for the church to be sustained through the change. We ask for the mission to continue. Amen.


## A Closing Prayer

Father, we bring this struggle to you. We ask for your wisdom, your strength, and your presence. We ask for the community to hold us. We ask for the hope that does not disappoint. Amen.
