# The Christian Who Doesn't Give: A South African's Honest Guide to the Person Who Sits in Church Every Sunday and Never Puts Anything in the Offering

## The Scene

The man in Randburg has been attending the Wednesday Bible study for two years. He has never given money. Not once. The leadership has noticed. The whispers have started. He has a job. He drives a decent car. But he does not give.

**2 Corinthians 9:6-7** — *"Remember this: Whoever sows sparingly will also reap sparingly, and whoever sows generously will also reap generously. Each of you should give what you have decided in your heart to give, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver."*

The South African Christian in the situations described above is not alone. The Bible has been speaking to these situations for two thousand years. The Wednesday Bible study in Parkmore has been hearing these passages read, discussed, and applied for ten years. What follows is a distillation of what the Bible says and what we have learned from the journey.


## What the Bible Says About Giving When You Have Nothing

2 Corinthians 8:12 says: 'For if the willingness is there, the gift is acceptable according to what one has, not according to what one does not have.' This is the key verse for the Christian who genuinely cannot give. The offering is measured by the heart, not the amount. The Christian who gives R5 from R500 with a joyful heart has given more in God's accounting than the Christian who gives R500 from R50,000 grudgingly.

## What the Bible Says About Giving When You Have Plenty

For the Christian who has plenty and does not give, the Bible is less gentle. Proverbs 21:13 says: 'Whoever shuts their ears to the cry of the poor will call out and not be answered.' The Christian who can give and chooses not to is making a choice that has spiritual consequences.

## What the Church Should Not Do

The church should not publish giving amounts. The church should not pressure people publicly or privately in ways that feel coercive. The church should not make announcements about the budget shortfall that imply that members are not pulling their weight. These tactics may produce money. They also produce resentment and destroy trust.

## What the Church Should Do

First: teach about giving consistently and biblically — not just when the budget is tight. Second: provide multiple giving options — cash, EFT, Snapscan, Zapper. Third: create a culture where giving is private. Fourth: have a pastoral conversation privately with the person who never gives, before making assumptions.

## A Prayer for the One Who Cannot Give

Father, we bring the Christian who has nothing to give. We bring the shame of the empty hand in the offering plate. We ask for the assurance that you are not disappointed. We ask for the gratitude for what is given to us. We ask for the day when we can give more. We ask for the church to be patient with us. Amen.


## A Closing Prayer

Father, we bring this struggle to you. We ask for your wisdom, your strength, and your presence. We ask for the community to hold us. We ask for the hope that does not disappoint. Amen.
