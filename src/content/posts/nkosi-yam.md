## Nkosi Yam

*Nkosi Yam* (Lord My God) is a traditional Xhosa hymn expressing deep reverence and love for God. Xhosa Christian music blends Western hymnody with traditional Xhosa musical elements, creating a unique sound of praise.

### Verse 1 (isiXhosa)
Nkosi yam, wena ungcwele
Ndinika ithuba lokubonga
Ndinika ithuba lokuvinga
Wena wedwa, Nkosi yam

### Chorus (Chorus)
Haleluya! Haleluya!
Nkosi yam ungcwele
Haleluya! Haleluya!
Igama lakho lihlekile

### Verse 2 (isiXhosa)
Umsindo wezulu uyavakala
Ilizwi lakho liyaphumza
Indlela yakho ayinakuhlambela
Singabathembi Nkosi

### Translation Reference
- *Nkosi* = Lord / God
- *Yam* = My
- *Ungcwele* = Holy / Sacred
- *Haleluya* = Hallelujah
- *Igama* = Name
