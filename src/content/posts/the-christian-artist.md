# Beauty, Art, and the Christian: A South African Christian's Guide to Creating Beauty in a World That Often Does Not Deserve It

## The Scene

The woman in Parkmore paints Johannesburg landscapes. She does not paint religious subjects. She does not know how to describe her work as Christian. She goes to church. She wonders if what she does is faithful.

**Genesis 1:31** — *"God saw all that he had made, and it was very good."*

The South African Christian in the situations described above is not alone. The Bible has been speaking to these situations for two thousand years. The Wednesday Bible study in Parkmore has been hearing these passages read, discussed, and applied for ten years. What follows is a distillation of what the Bible says and what we have learned from the journey.


## What the Bible Says About Art and Beauty

Exodus 35:30-35 describes the artisans — Bezalel and Oholiab — whom God filled with the Spirit to do artistic work: 'He has filled them with skill to do all kinds of work as craftsmen, designers, embroiderers in blue, purple and scarlet yarn and fine linen, and weavers.' Art is a spiritual gift. The artist who creates beauty is doing holy work.

## The Question of Christian Art

Christian art is not only art that depicts biblical scenes or includes explicit religious content. Christian art is art made by Christian artists — people who see through Christian eyes, who are shaped by Christian imagination, who bring a Christian sensibility to whatever they paint, photograph, write, or compose.

## The Johannesburg Landscape as a Sacred Subject

The Johannesburg landscape — the jacarandas in October, the mine dumps on the horizon, the informal settlements visible from the highway, the cranes over Sandton — is a sacred subject. It is the place where God has placed the artist. The Christian artist who paints Johannesburg is painting a holy landscape.

## A Prayer for the Christian Artist

Father, we bring the artist who creates beauty in your world. We bring the painter, the poet, the photographer, the musician. Fill them with your Spirit as you filled Bezalel. Give them the skill that comes from you. Give them the courage to show the world as they see it — beautiful and broken. Use their art to point to your kingdom. Amen.


## A Closing Prayer

Father, we bring this struggle to you. We ask for your wisdom, your strength, and your presence. We ask for the community to hold us. We ask for the hope that does not disappoint. Amen.
