# The Christian in Blue: A South African Police Officer's Honest Guide to Justice, Use of Force, and Faith in One of the Hardest Jobs in the Country

## The Scene

The constable in Springs has been in the SAPS for eight years. He joined because he wanted to serve. He has seen things that have changed him. He has made decisions in the moment that he is not sure about. He goes to church. He is not sure how to reconcile the two.

**2 Corinthians 12:9** — *"My grace is sufficient for you, for my power is made perfect in weakness."*

The South African Christian in the situations described above is not alone. The Bible has been speaking to these situations for two thousand years. The Wednesday Bible study in Parkmore has been hearing these passages read, discussed, and applied for ten years. What follows is a distillation of what the Bible says and what we have learned from the journey.


## What Romans 13 Says to Police Officers

Romans 13:1-4 describes the role of the state authority: 'They are God's servants, agents of wrath to bring punishment on the wrongdoer.' The police officer who takes this seriously is not a thug with a badge. They are a servant of God's justice. This is a high calling.

## The Systemic Problem

The South African Police Service has a serious problem with corruption, brutality, and inefficiency. The Christian officer who works within this system faces a specific dilemma: how do I uphold justice in an unjust system? The answer is: imperfectly, courageously, and with accountability.

## The Use of Force Question

The Christian police officer who has used force in the line of duty faces a conscience question that the church has rarely addressed. The Bible does not forbid the use of force in self-defence or the defence of others. But the use of force must be proportional, necessary, and accountable.

## A Prayer for the Police Officer

Father, we bring the officer in blue who serves a broken system. We bring the one who has seen too much. We bring the one who has made decisions in the moment that they are not sure about. Give us the courage to act justly. Give us the wisdom to know when to use force and when not to. Give us the integrity that is accountable. Give us the support we need to keep going. Amen.


## A Closing Prayer

Father, we bring this struggle to you. We ask for your wisdom, your strength, and your presence. We ask for the community to hold us. We ask for the hope that does not disappoint. Amen.
