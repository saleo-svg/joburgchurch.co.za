# The Garden and the Soul: A South African Christian's Honest Guide to Growing Things, the Soil, and What the Bible Learns from the Garden

## The Scene

The man in Springs retired and started a vegetable garden. He did not expect to find God in it. He has been surprised.

**Genesis 1:31** — *"God saw all that he had made, and it was very good."*

The South African Christian in the situations described above is not alone. The Bible has been speaking to these situations for two thousand years. The Wednesday Bible study in Parkmore has been hearing these passages read, discussed, and applied for ten years. What follows is a distillation of what the Bible says and what we have learned from the journey.


## What Genesis 2 Says About Gardens

Genesis 2:8 says: 'Now the Lord God had planted a garden in the east, and there he put the man he had formed.' God planted a garden before God placed humans in it. The garden is not an accident. It is intentional. The human who gardens is participating in something ancient and sacred.

## What Gardening Teaches About Patience

Galatians 6:9 says: 'Let us not become weary in doing good, for at the proper time we will reap a harvest if we do not give up.' The farmer who plants does not expect to harvest tomorrow. The Christian who has learned to garden has learned this lesson in a way that no book can teach. The garden that takes three months to produce food is a three-month lesson in faith.

## The South African Garden and the Water Crisis

The South African gardener in 2026 faces the water crisis. Gardening in Johannesburg requires water discipline — rainwater harvesting, drought-tolerant plants, mulching. This discipline is not just practical. It is a spiritual discipline of stewardship.

## A Prayer for the Gardener

Father, we bring the garden that you planted before you placed us in it. We bring the seeds that are slow to grow. We bring the plants that need rain. We bring the soil that is tired. Teach us through the garden the patience you are teaching us through life. Teach us through the seeds the faith that trusts the harvest. Teach us through the soil the humility of those who tend rather than manufacture. Amen.


## A Closing Prayer

Father, we bring this struggle to you. We ask for your wisdom, your strength, and your presence. We ask for the community to hold us. We ask for the hope that does not disappoint. Amen.
