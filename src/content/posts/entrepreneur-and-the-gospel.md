# The Entrepreneur and the Gospel: A South African Christian's Guide to Business, Risk, Faith, and the God Who Blesses Enterprise

## The Scene

The man in Sandton quit his corporate job to start a logistics business. He has been running it for two years. He has had good months and terrible months. He has made promises to his staff about bonuses that he is not sure he can keep. He is the only Christian in his industry that he knows of.

**James 1:5** — *"If any of you lacks wisdom, you should ask God, who gives generously to all without finding fault, and it will be given to you."*

The South African Christian in the situations described above is not alone. The Bible has been speaking to these situations for two thousand years. The Wednesday Bible study in Parkmore has been hearing these passages read, discussed, and applied for ten years. What follows is a distillation of what the Bible says and what we have learned from the journey.


## What the Bible Says About Enterprise

Proverbs is the Bible's book of business wisdom. 'The plans of the diligent lead to profit as surely as haste leads to poverty' (21:5). 'Honest scales and balances are from the Lord' (16:11). 'The wealth of the rich is their fortified city' (18:11). These verses take enterprise seriously as a legitimate Christian activity.

## The Temptation of Success

The entrepreneur who becomes successful faces a specific temptation: the success was not mine. The success was God's blessing. This is true. But it is also true that the success was the result of skill, risk, and hard work. The entrepreneur who credits God for the success but does not give God credit for the failures has an incomplete theology.

## The Christian Employer's Responsibilities

The Christian who employs people has specific responsibilities. Proverbs 22:16 says: 'The rich exploit the poor, and the borrower is slave to the lender.' The employer who underpays, overworks, or exploits their employees is exploiting the vulnerable. This is not consistent with the Christian faith.

## A Prayer for the Entrepreneur

Father, we bring the business that you have given us to build. We ask for the wisdom to run it well. We ask for the integrity to pay fairly and treat employees well. We ask for the courage to be honest about the failures as well as the successes. We ask for the generosity to give back to the community that made the business possible. Amen.


## A Closing Prayer

Father, we bring this struggle to you. We ask for your wisdom, your strength, and your presence. We ask for the community to hold us. We ask for the hope that does not disappoint. Amen.
