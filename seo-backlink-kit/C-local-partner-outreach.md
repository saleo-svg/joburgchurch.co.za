# C — Local Partner Outreach Kit (Sandton / Parkmore / Greater Johannesburg)

> 目标: 跟**真正的本地组织**合作，互相提及/链接。这是最有价值的链接方式，
> 因为 Google 把"真实组织认可你"视为最强信号。

---

## 🎯 Outreach cadence

| Week | Action | Target count |
|------|--------|--------------|
| 1 | Send 5 introduction emails (template below) | 5 |
| 2 | Follow-up on non-responders | 3-5 |
| 3 | Phone calls for warm leads | 2-3 |
| 4 | First meeting / 联合活动 kickoff | 1 |
| 6 | First link or co-event | 1-2 |
| 12 | Quarterly review of who replied and where links landed | — |

**Conversion rate** for cold outreach in SA church context: 10-25%. So 20 emails → 2-5 partnerships.

## 📧 Outreach templates

### Template 1 — Cold intro email (pastor → pastor)

```
Subject: Wednesday Bible study in Parkmore — quick hello from a neighbour

Dear Pastor [Name],

My name is Sim, and I lead a small Bible-centred community in Parkmore,
Sandton that meets online every Wednesday at 7:30pm. We have been running
in Parkmore for [N] years and serve people from Parkmore, Sandton,
Randburg, Midrand, and surrounding areas.

I am writing because I admire what [church name] is doing for [their
specific program / community they mentioned], and I would love to find
a way for our two congregations to support each other in some small way.

A few ideas, if any are of interest:

  • A quarterly joint prayer or fellowship evening
  • Referring young families who move into Sandton to your services,
    and vice versa (we do not run a building per se — we are a
    distributed community)
  • Cross-posting your events on our community blog, and you ours

If you have 20 minutes for a coffee in the next few weeks, I would be
honoured. I can come to you, or we can meet somewhere central in
Parkmore.

Either way — thank you for the work you do in our city.

Blessings,
Sim
Johannesburg Bible Study Church
Parkmore, Sandton
+27 77 487 1295
joburgchurch.co.za
```

## 🤝 What to ask for when a partner agrees

Always negotiate a **link back**. Polite, professional ways to phrase:

- "Would you mind listing our Bible study as a referral option on
   your chaplaincy page? A small link near the bottom is fine —
   we would happily return the favour on our site."
- "I will add your organisation to our community partners section
   in the footer. Should I link to your homepage or a specific
   program page?"
- "Could your events team list our Korean class on the community
   calendar? In exchange, I will list your next youth event on
   ours."

**Never demand** placement. Always offer to reciprocate.

---

## 🛡️ What NOT to do

- DO NOT spam a list of 30 churches by sending 30 copies of the
  same pitch with one field changed.
- DO NOT propose "link exchanges" — Google treats this as a link
  scheme and can devalue both sites.
- DO NOT pay for any placement.
- DO NOT pretend to be a different organisation when writing.
- DO NOT use registration-only email aliases (mailinator, yopmail)
  for outreach — it signals spam.
