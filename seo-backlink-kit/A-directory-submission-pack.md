## 📋 Per-directory submission steps

### 1. Google Business Profile (GBP)

URL: https://www.google.com/business/

Steps:
1. Sign in with the **church Google account** (建议新建一个 church@joburgchurch.co.za 用 Gmail)
2. Click "Manage now" → "Add your business"
3. **Business name**: `Johannesburg Bible Study Church`
4. **Category**: `Church` → add `Religious organisation` as secondary
5. **Address**: as above; toggle "I deliver to customers" OFF
6. **Service area**: Parkmore, Sandton (only if no physical storefront)
7. **Phone**: +27 77 487 1295
8. **Website**: https://joburgchurch.co.za
9. **Hours**: Wed 19:30; Sun 14:00
10. **Description**: paste Description 1 above
11. **Photo upload**: 推荐加 1 张外景图（church/community 类）做 cover
12. Submit → **Google sends postcard with PIN** (4-14 days)
13. Enter PIN 后 listing 公开

**Critical**: After verification, **每周** update GBP with a post (event, photo, quote). GBP listing with active posts 排名明显高。

### 2. Bing Places

URL: https://www.bingplaces.com/

Same NAP. Bing 不发 postcard — 用 phone 或 email 验证即可。

### 3. Apple Maps Connect

URL: https://mapsconnect.apple.com/

Sign in with Apple ID → add place → wait 5-7 days review.

### 4-22. Tier 2-4

每个目录都是同样的 NAP 填表流程。建议保持一个 **NAP master doc**（你站 root 下 `admin/NAP.md`），每次提交直接复制。
