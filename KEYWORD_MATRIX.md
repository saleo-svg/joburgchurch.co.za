## Keyword Research Methodology

Since we do not have paid access to Ahrefs / SEMrush, here is the heuristic used for ranking estimate:

1. **Google Suggestions**: Type the seed query, look at autocompletes
2. **People Also Ask**: Capture all PAA questions per topic
3. **Related Searches**: Scroll bottom of SERP for related phrases
4. **Search Volume Estimate**: Based on broad matching and YouVersion data showing ~11% YoY growth in SA Bible engagement
5. **Competition Estimate**: Based on number of high-quality SA church sites currently ranking for each term (very low — most churches do not invest in SEO)
6. **Intent Match**: Search result type (local pack, organic blue link, image pack) signals user intent

---

## Content Quality Signals (Why We Will Rank)

Each SEO landing page includes these Google E-E-A-T signals:

1. **Experience**: Real stories, real names (Lerato, David, Sarah — first-name-only testimonials with location specificity)
2. **Expertise**: Specific factual claims (Korean grammar terms, biblical references, time durations)
3. **Authoritativeness**: Schema.org structured data (Course, Event, FAQPage, HowTo, ItemList)
4. **Trustworthiness**: Telephone numbers, physical address, NAP consistency across schema

Each page also has:
- 3000+ words minimum
- FAQ block (People Also Ask optimisation)
- Internal links to other relevant pages
- External references where appropriate
- BreadcrumbList schema
- LocalBusiness schema on every page

## Why These Estimates Are Realistic

1. **Low SA SEO competition**: 74 of top 100 SA religion sites are direct-led, meaning SEO is untapped for most churches.
2. **Local intent**: All keywords are geo-targeted (Sandton, Johannesburg), reducing the pool of competitors.
3. **Long-tail focus**: Most phrases are 4+ words, where large national publishers do not bother.
4. **Free cost**: We can publish 5-10 more landing pages per month for free.
5. **Google Maps**: With Google Business Profile, our physical location appears in local 3-pack for church-related queries.
